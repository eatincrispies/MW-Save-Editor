EA Trax Songs
=============

Each playlist entry is 8 bytes: the song number (4 bytes), then the play setting (1 byte): 00 Off, 01 Menu, 02 Racing, 03 All.
The entries are in playlist order, and every song has to appear once. The play mode is 4 bytes: 0 Sequential, 1 Random.
