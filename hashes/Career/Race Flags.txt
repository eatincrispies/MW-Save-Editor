Race Flags
==========

A 4-byte value at +04 of a race record. Add the bits together to turn on more than one.

Flag                  Bit Value  PC/PS2 Bytes  360/GC Bytes
--------------------  ---------  ------------  ------------
Quick Race completed  00000001   01 00 00 00   00 00 00 01
Career completed      00000002   02 00 00 00   00 00 00 02
Quick Race unlocked   00000004   04 00 00 00   00 00 00 04
Career unlocked       00000008   08 00 00 00   00 00 00 08
Online unlocked       00000010   10 00 00 00   00 00 00 10
