Milestone Types
===============

Each milestone record starts with its type. The goal is the 4-byte float at +0C. Speed traps have their own records and aren't in this list.

Milestone                Goal at +0C                Type      PC/PS2 Bytes  360/GC Bytes
-----------------------  -------------------------  --------  ------------  ------------
Trade Paint              Police vehicles tagged     850A64BC  BC 64 0A 85   85 0A 64 BC
Bounty                   Bounty in one pursuit      2377E50D  0D E5 77 23   23 77 E5 0D
Pursuit Evasion          Seconds to evade in        5392E4FD  FD E4 92 53   53 92 E4 FD
Pursuit Time Challenge   Seconds the pursuit lasts  033FA23A  3A A2 3F 03   03 3F A2 3A
Cost to State Challenge  Cost to state              FD989A3A  3A 9A 98 FD   FD 98 9A 3A
Roadblock Challenge      Roadblocks dodged          EB45F99D  9D F9 45 EB   EB 45 F9 9D
Spike Strip Challenge    Spike strips dodged        CDF36FC2  C2 6F F3 CD   CD F3 6F C2
Infractions Challenge    Infractions                A61CAC24  24 AC 1C A6   A6 1C AC 24
