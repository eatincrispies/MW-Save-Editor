Career Flags
============

A 4-byte value at the Career flags offset. Add the bits together to turn on more than one.

Flag                      Bit Value  PC/PS2 Bytes  360/GC Bytes
------------------------  ---------  ------------  ------------
Career started            00000001   01 00 00 00   00 00 00 01
Has Rap Sheet             00000010   10 00 00 00   00 00 00 10
Intro done                00000020   20 00 00 00   00 00 00 20
Game over                 00000800   00 08 00 00   00 00 08 00
Busted at least once      00001000   00 10 00 00   00 00 10 00
Career beaten             00004000   00 40 00 00   00 00 40 00
Special challenge beaten  00008000   00 80 00 00   00 00 80 00
Challenge Series beaten   00010000   00 00 01 00   00 01 00 00
Castrol Ford GT           00040000   00 00 04 00   00 04 00 00
Epic car awarded          00100000   00 00 10 00   00 10 00 00
