Milestone States
================

A complete milestone also needs its recorded value at +10 to meet the goal. Each rank's challenge counter is the number of complete milestones and speed traps, so the Races > Milestones tab is the safer way.

[Milestone state: 1 byte at +08]
State                            Value
-------------------------------  -----
Locked                           01
Available                        02
Done, waiting for you to escape  03
Complete                         04

[Speed trap flags: 2 bytes at +00, add them together]
State                            Value
-------------------------------  -----
Unlocked                         0001
Active                           0002
Complete                         0004
Knocked over                     0008
