Infractions
===========

Each count is 2 bytes inside a career car record. The fine is what the game charges per infraction.

Infraction                Unserved  Served  Fine
------------------------  --------  ------  -----
Speeding                  +18       +28     150
Excessive Speeding        +1A       +2A     350
Reckless Driving          +1C       +2C     1,000
Ramming a Police Vehicle  +1E       +2E     350
Hit and Run               +20       +30     300
Damage to Property        +22       +32     100
Resisting Arrest          +24       +34     300
Driving off Roadway       +26       +36     75
