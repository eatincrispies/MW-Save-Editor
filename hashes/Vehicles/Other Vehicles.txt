Other Vehicles
==============

The Vehicle Key goes at +08 of a car record and the Frontend Key at +04. See Save Layout\Car Records.txt.
Anything marked May crash really can crash the game, so keep a backup.
The ones marked Not in Add Car are in the game's saves but aren't meant to be driven.
