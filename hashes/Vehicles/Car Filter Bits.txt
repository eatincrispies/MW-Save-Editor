Car Filter Bits
===============

A 4-byte value at +0C of a car record. It decides which lists the car shows up in. A normal career car is 000F0002.

Flag             Bit Value  PC/PS2 Bytes  360/GC Bytes
---------------  ---------  ------------  ------------
Car Lot          00000001   01 00 00 00   00 00 00 01
Career garage    00000002   02 00 00 00   00 00 00 02
My Cars          00000004   04 00 00 00   00 00 00 04
Bonus            00000008   08 00 00 00   00 00 00 08
Preset           00000010   10 00 00 00   00 00 00 10
Debug / hidden   00000020   20 00 00 00   00 00 00 20
Pink slip        00000040   40 00 00 00   00 00 00 40
Region: America  00010000   00 00 01 00   00 01 00 00
Region: Europe   00020000   00 00 02 00   00 02 00 00
Region: Japan    00040000   00 00 04 00   00 04 00 00
Region: Debug    00080000   00 00 08 00   00 08 00 00
